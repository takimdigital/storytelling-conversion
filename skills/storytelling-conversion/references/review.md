# Audit and editing through the eight principles

Inspect the actual work, not whether its author mentions storytelling terminology. Fix the largest failure while preserving effective voice, offer, language, and scope.

## Eight-principle audit

| Principle | Find in the draft | Repair when missing or weak |
|---|---|---|
| Predictive processing | A recognizable pattern, relevant uncertainty, and a supported turn/payoff | Replace the predictable claim or confusing teaser with a question the body genuinely resolves |
| Establishing shot | Where, who, mood, focus in the opening | Supply necessary context or replace a long backstory with the consequential moment |
| Word pictures | The intended mental image and its relation to the takeaway | Replace abstractions with observable detail; remove an irrelevant image dominating recall |
| Structure | Named structure's actual beats and the intended feeling | Reorder for causal movement; develop the absent catalyst, consequence, reframe, or resolution |
| Chekhov's gun | A job for each line and a payoff for emphasized setups | Cut, connect, substantiate, or resolve; preserve relevant terms and proof |
| Villain | Specific obstacle, connected consequences, external forces, attempts, and failure mechanism | Replace a vague problem label with recognizable mechanics supported by context |
| Sugar coating | Medicine, sugar, and a carrier that delivers both | Put the information into a relevant demonstration, decision, conflict, personality, or discovery |
| Bookending | Emotional endpoints and a transformed opening element | Earn a changed meaning; complete the argument rather than echo a phrase decoratively |

For each substantial failure, report the passage, principle, effect on the reader, and concrete revision. Mark a principle not needed when the assignment genuinely does not call for it; do not mark it irrelevant merely because it takes work to apply.

## Open-loop ledger

For each important setup, record:

```text
Setup or emphasized detail → reader expectation → payoff location → action
```

Actions: retain, connect, move, resolve, or remove. Distinguish a relevant fact doing immediate work from an unresolved promise. Check that closing the main loop does not quietly abandon another conspicuous detail.

## Line-job edit

Name the job of each line: desire, objection, proof, legitimate urgency, orientation, causal development, or material condition. Remove empty motivation, repeated claims, unrelated prestige, and competing asks. Strengthen a necessary line that is vague rather than deleting its purpose.

Do not measure improvement by word count. A missing circumstance, consequence, example, or term may need adding. A strong sentence should survive unchanged. Accurate information about another offer still changes scope when inserted into a focused piece.

## Three practical inspections

1. **Opening alone:** Is the subject intelligible and the question interesting? Can the reader identify where, who, mood, and focus?
2. **Heard rather than seen:** Read aloud, use available text-to-speech, or mentally hear the piece. What image remains? Does it represent the intended idea? Where only summary language remains, rewrite it.
3. **Ending removed:** What necessary resolution or emotional change disappears? If only a repeated phrase is lost, the callback is decorative. If the ending makes a new unsupported promise, it breaks coherence.

These are editorial inspections, not claims that real people have been tested. Report actual reader feedback only when it exists.

## Common false repairs

- Adding a question mark to a feature summary and calling it a hook.
- Adding a clock, smell, or colored object without making it relevant to the setup or payoff.
- Naming a framework without building its beats.
- Naming stress as the villain without showing what sustains it or why attempted fixes fail.
- Making the stakes extreme without evidence.
- Putting a dry feature list after a dramatic opening with no causal connection.
- Calling a repeated slogan a bookend when nothing changes in meaning.
- Cutting conditions so the copy looks cleaner but communicates a different offer.
- Recommending a new post when the relevant answer already exists and needs emphasis or repair.

## Delivery

For a narrow edit, return the revised passage and a short reason when useful. For a full audit, prioritize a small number of consequential failures and provide usable repairs. Separate factual uncertainties from craft defects. A persuasive revision still needs accurate claims; an accurate revision still needs the requested craft.
