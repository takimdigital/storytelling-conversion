# Examples and prompt recipes

The examples are authored illustrations using fictional products. They are not testimonials, observed customer events, or results of an automated writing trial.

## See the method working together

The [production guide](../skills/storytelling-conversion/references/production.md) contains:

- A complete email about a document-review tool, with its opening uncertainty, establishing shot, yellow-comment image, causal turn, useful lesson, relevant product mechanism, and transformed closing return.
- A four-step obstacle build for a fictional team managing stock requests: connected consequences, sustaining external forces, attempted fixes, and their failure mechanism.
- A focused repair-shop edit showing when a direct explanation serves the placement better than an extended narrative.
- A launch-sequence map connecting each narrative role to its local payoff and the eventual offer.

Read the design notes as well as the copy. The method should transfer to a different business without copying the example's colored object, phrases, or scenario.

## Write a new email

```text
Use $storytelling-conversion to write one email for this business.

Business and verified offer: [facts]
Audience and current awareness: [context]
Situation or audience language we actually know: [material]
Intended action: [action]
Voice, language, and length: [constraints]

Apply the eight-principle method. Deliver the subject and email first.
Keep any important assumptions separate from the finished copy.
```

The useful work includes choosing the relevant obstacle, emotional movement, medicine and sugar, structure, opening promise, meaningful image, and resolution. A list of features after a generic greeting is not the intended result.

## Audit and improve an existing piece

```text
Use $storytelling-conversion to audit this existing page section.
Identify the most consequential failures through the eight principles,
then give a revised section. Preserve the offer, intended audience,
effective voice, and material terms. Decide what to retain, remove,
clarify, reorder, or develop.

Business facts: [facts]
Current section: [copy]
```

An audit should locate actual passages, explain the reader effect, and make concrete repairs. It may find that a vivid aside dominates recall, a promise never pays off, or a problem needs a more specific mechanism. It should not equate improvement with either deleting or adding words.

## Discover a useful marketing direction

```text
Use $storytelling-conversion to decide what communication we should
work on next. Inspect existing material before proposing another asset.

Business and audience: [context]
Existing content and user path: [material]
Audience observations, if available: [evidence]
Time, budget, and operational limits: [constraints]

Find the narrative or understanding gap. Recommend a focused edit,
new piece, or sequence and explain its audience progression.
Include a concrete concept or copy, not only a list of channels.
```

For a sequence, identify what each item changes in the reader's understanding or emotional state, what it pays off locally, and how it contributes to the final resolution. For an edit, name the gap the existing material can repair.

## Preserve a narrow request

```text
Use $storytelling-conversion to improve only this opening sentence.
The subject must remain intelligible, and the body must deliver
whatever the revised opening promises. Keep the remaining copy intact.

Opening and body: [copy]
```

The output can be one revised line. The skill does not need to display a full design worksheet to make a small repair.

## Work in French

```text
Utilise $storytelling-conversion pour réécrire ce courriel en français
canadien naturel. Préserve les faits, l'offre et le ton de la marque.
Construis une situation reconnaissable, une progression qui donne
envie de lire et une fin qui donne un sens nouveau à l'ouverture.

Public et objectif : [contexte]
Faits vérifiés : [faits]
Courriel actuel : [texte]
```

Recreate the narrative experience idiomatically; do not translate a pun or sensory expression mechanically. Match the facts and conditions across languages.

## Direct questions remain direct

If a reader asks when a price is confirmed, and the verified repair-shop process confirms it after inspection and before work begins, answer that question plainly. That placement needs clarity, not a miniature campaign.

## Human review

Use the eight-principle [editing guide](../skills/storytelling-conversion/references/review.md) to inspect the actual piece. Look for the intended prediction and payoff, orientation, mental image, structure, purposeful details, specific obstacle, medicine/sugar pairing, and changed meaning at the end.

The [scenario fixtures](../evals/cases.json) remain available for voluntary future checks. Authored examples and an instruction audit do not establish creative superiority or business results.
